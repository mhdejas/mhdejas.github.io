Spatial atlas

Kozhikode Atlas

A source-documented interactive atlas of Kozhikode, combining terrain, land cover, satellite comparison and environmental context.

LeafletThree.jsSentinel-2Kerala
Methods and limits +
Purpose
Bring district geography and selected Earth-observation layers together in a browsable atlas.
Methods
The repository documents Copernicus DEM elevation and slope, ESA WorldCover, a January–March 2019 versus 2025 Sentinel-2 comparison, Dynamic World built-up signals and landslide context.
Evidence
The urban layer is an exploratory candidate signal, not a confirmed building inventory or validated area estimate. No independent ground-reference accuracy is reported.
Data rights
Source code is MIT-licensed; imagery and datasets have separate terms. Redistribution rights for the displayed GSI/KSDMA susceptibility layer remain unresolved. Urban comparison method · GSI data note.

Experimental model

EARTHSIM-Ω

A reproducible research prototype exploring terrain, rainfall-runoff and flood mapping in Kozhikode—not an operational warning system.

PythonSentinel-1HydrologyFastAPI
Methods and limits +
Methods
The repository documents 30 m terrain/hydrology, Sentinel-1 flood mapping, Sentinel-2 analysis, a local 2-D solver and an experimental forecast cycle.
Reported test
For one held-out 10 August 2019 comparison, the report gives a calibrated CSI of 0.019 versus 0.014 for the default solver. Its 95% block-bootstrap interval (0.001–0.036) leaves the improvement statistically unresolved.
Limit
The report notes poor flood-footprint skill, incomplete independent validation and limitations in the physical model. It is not for emergency, operational or public-warning decisions.
Source status
These are repository-reported research results, not independently rerun here. The repository has no stated reuse license.

Earth-observation workflow

Agricultural Health Alert System

A crop-monitoring workflow that compares vegetation-index signals with historical baselines. The CV reports a live Sentinel-2 test over farmland in Maharashtra.

PythonEarth EngineSentinel-1/2NDVI
Methods and limits +
Purpose
Flag significant vegetation-index drops against multi-year historical baselines for further inspection.
Methods
The CV describes Sentinel-2 and Landsat 8/9 imagery, ESA WorldCover masking, spatial smoothing and anomaly vectorisation. The project provides GeoJSON, CSV, PDF and email alert outputs.
Test result
On live Sentinel-2 imagery over farmland in Maharashtra, the CV reports 10 alert zones with mean NDVI values from 0.057 to 0.162.
Limit
An NDVI anomaly is a screening signal, not a field-confirmed crop diagnosis. It should be reviewed with local evidence before action.

Coastal studyIndependent research · exploratory

Looking at Kerala Beaches with Real Satellite Images and Sea-Floor Data

The expanded study combines Landsat waterline observations from 1990–2025 with quality-screened satellite-derived bathymetry. The CV identifies the working paper as Version 6 (2026); the Zenodo record is versioned separately as v2.0.

233OpenStreetMap beach features in the expanded study
1,444Accepted transect–epoch crossings
157Estimable feature-window bathymetry medians after quality checks

Exploratory and not peer reviewed. The work does not establish validated erosion rates or show that seabed shape alone explains erosion. Valid tide-gauge observations bracketed 13 of 55 acquisition times; no beach-level tide correction was applied because local survey and vertical-datum links were unavailable.

Geospatial investigation

Why is Wayanad so vulnerable to extreme rainfall?

The article combines 30 m terrain context with one 12-day Sentinel-1A before/after pair. It reports a median slope of 22.6° in the selected terrain window and 0.195 km² of radar pixels flagged at the middle threshold.

The radar flags indicate unusual signal change, not a confirmed landslide footprint. One image pair cannot separate landslide-related change from monsoon moisture or vegetation effects.

Research boundary

A screening signal, not a hazard map.

The article’s threshold comparison is exploratory. It does not classify landslides, map runout, or predict future slope failure.

Open mapping

Make the map
more useful.

I contributed to SLIM Zambia, a HOT Tasking Manager project for climate resilience and disaster risk reduction, by tracing visible rural access tracks and compound driveways from satellite imagery.

Across nine task areas, I added 10 road features and made four small geometry adjustments where driveways joined existing roads. Edits were submitted for review; unclear roads and connections were left unmapped.
